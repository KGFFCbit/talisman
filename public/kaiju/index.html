Honey Badger Kaiju

A giant armored honey badger, standing in Tokyo Bay, entirely unbothered.

It started as a joke request: a honey badger destroying Tokyo. The first render came back with the badger swimming. Thirty-two renders later it stands up, wears armor, and has exactly two hands.

Art: FLUX.1 schnell, running locally in ComfyUI · Direction and layout: Claude

The posters

Five finished posters. The made-up Japanese the model scribbled on the drafts has been removed, or covered with real title boxes on the first one.

The drafts

Everything the model returned

Thirty-two renders in four rounds, exactly as generated. Some have three hands. The Japanese on the posters is invented by the model and does not say anything. Click any image to enlarge.

Round 1 · Cinematic · 4 renders

The brief said wading. The badger heard swimming.

Round 2 · Cinematic, again · 8 renders

Tokyo Tower and Skytree added, black bars gone. The collapsed bridge span never showed up.

Round 3 · Three poster styles · 12 renders

Vintage paper, ukiyo-e print, bold vector. Standing up this time.

Round 4 · Ukiyo-e redraw · 8 renders

Redrawn to get rid of a third hand: one prompt spells out two arms, the other hides the far arm.

How it was made

FLUX.1 schnell running locally in ComfyUI on a single NVIDIA GPU, four steps per image. The twelve poster drafts rendered in under two minutes. Prompts, cropping, the title boxes and this page were directed with Claude; the 27-minute screen recording was cut down to the video above.
