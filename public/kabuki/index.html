Kabuki Goat

One prompt. Four goats. Pick your lead.

I asked for one kabuki goat and got four. Same words every time, four different characters: a calm lead, a villain with a red beard, a trickster, and an elder.

Art: FLUX.1 schnell, running locally in ComfyUI · Prompt and layout: Claude

The cast

Four renders, one prompt

Straight from the model, no retouching. The lettering in the corners is invented by the model and does not say anything. Click any image to enlarge.

How it was made

FLUX.1 schnell running locally in ComfyUI on a single NVIDIA GPU: 1024 by 1280 pixels, four steps per image, one prompt, four seeds. Claude wrote the prompt. The four images are exactly as generated.

Read the prompt

Japanese ukiyo-e woodblock print in the style of Utagawa Kunisada kabuki actor portraits: a majestic horned goat standing on four legs in a dramatic kabuki mie pose, head turned to face the viewer with a fierce crossed-eyed glare. Bold red kumadori face paint stripes down its white face, long flowing white and red lion-dance mane, curved horns with gold ornaments, an ornate gold-embroidered silk robe draped over its back. Painted pine-tree stage backdrop, kabuki curtain stripes in black, persimmon orange and green, flat colors, bold black outlines, aged paper texture. No text, no lettering, no watermark.
